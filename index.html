    How to play

    • •
      Tap: jumpTap again in the air to double jump (about 3 blocks)
    • ✋
      Hold: stop, keep holding: reverseYou run forward on your own. Hold to stop, keep holding to back up. Hold and slide to turn on the spot
    • ↔
      Swipe: turnLeft or right anywhere on screen
    • ⇆
      Double-tap a screen edge: dodge that wayTwo dodges, then a quick reload. Tap the clock for the map and menu
    • ↓
      Swipe down: grabThe block you grab becomes your active block
    • ↑
      Swipe up: throwHarder swipe, further throw. Red ring = locked on

    Knock down their towers, grab one of their 4 flags and get it into the glowing gateway in your fort. First to 4 flags wins, or most flags when time runs out. Touch your own loose flag to send it home. Black bomb boxes blast everything nearby, icy freeze boxes stop everything in time for 3 seconds (robots slide), glowing purple homing boxes chase whoever you're locked on to. Throw a cow or sheep and it bowls robots over like pins.
